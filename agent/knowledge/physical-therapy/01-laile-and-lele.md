# Laile and Lele

## What Laile is

Laile (莱乐) is a home physical-therapy app with a strong calisthenics side. The phone's camera checks that exercises are really done, and done well, and Lele, a friendly voice coach, guides each session and checks in between them.

Everything in Laile is a **program**. There are three kinds:

- **Physio programs** come from a physiotherapist or doctor. They give you a code (it looks like LAI-XXXXXX). Lele drafts the program from your condition and your physio checks and signs it. Your physio can ask Lele to change it or try a new approach at any time, and only your physio can end it.
- **Lele's plans** are built by Lele from what you tell it: your goals, how much time you have, how much you move now, and anything that hurts.
- **Laile programs** come from Laile's catalog: calisthenics (Ten-minute strength, First push-up, Squat strong, Core foundations), mobility (Shoulder mobility), and after-physio programs (Strong knees for life).

There are also quick sessions of 2 to 7 minutes, like Desk reset or Morning mobility, and live classes you can join with other people.

## Who Lele is

Lele (乐乐) is Laile's coach and assistant. 乐 means joy; it is the "le" in Laile.

- During a session, Lele is the voice that counts with you, times your holds, and listens when you say how something feels.
- Between sessions, Lele checks in: a morning "how are you feeling?" while you're in physio, reminders for your medicines, a nudge on quiet days, and a question about what's getting in the way if you miss a few.
- You can chat with Lele in the Lele tab, or tap the phone icon to talk out loud.
- Lele works for Laile and alongside your physio. Lele is not a doctor or physiotherapist. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or give advice about doses. Your physio decides your physio program.

## Getting started

- **Have a code from your physio?** Enter it at sign-up, or in Programs → I have a code.
- **No code?** Lele asks a few questions: what you'd like to get out of it, how many minutes most days, how much you move now, anywhere that hurts or needs care, and one safety question. Then it builds your plan.
- If you mention an injury or pain, Lele keeps your plan gentle and suggests seeing a physiotherapist.

## Your first session is a check-in

The first session of any program is a check-in, not a test:

- You do each move once, at a comfortable effort, and tap "That's enough" when it feels right.
- The camera measures your reps, holds and how far your joints move.
- Lele sets your targets from it: about 70% of your best reps or hold, so each session is a working set, not a max test.
- A rep counts at 90% of the range you reached, if that's less than the usual target.
- In a physio program, targets are only ever made easier than your physio's dose, never harder.
- Re-check every couple of weeks from the Progress tab, and Lele resets your targets.

## How a Laile session works

1. **Before starting:** you rate your pain from 0 to 10 (physio programs).
2. **Camera setup:** Lele tells you where to put the phone. Most exercises need a side view.
3. **Your turn:** Lele explains the move, then says "In your own time". There's no countdown; start when you're ready.
4. **Exercising:** the camera tracks your joints and counts only reps that reach the target. Holds are timed only while you are in position.
5. **Talking to Lele:** you can speak at any time. "Feels fine", "it's pulling", "that's sharp" or "something clicked" are all understood. Pain above your physio's limit stops the set. Warning signs end the session with advice to get help.
6. **Between sets:** Lele asks how that felt.
7. **Afterwards:** you rate your pain again. In a physio program your physio can watch your session live and sees the results.

## What the camera measures

- **Knee bend (flexion):** how far your knee bends, in degrees. 0° is straight.
- **Knee straightening gap:** how far short of fully straight your knee stays. Smaller is better.
- **Arm lift:** how high your arm goes when you raise it forward, in degrees. 180° is straight up.
- **Reps and hold times:** counted by the camera, not typed in.
- **Form:** for example, whether your knee stays straight during a straight-leg raise, or your hips stay in line during a plank.

**Privacy:**
- Video never leaves your phone; only the measurements are saved.
- What you say to Lele may be sent to Laile's servers so Lele can reply.

## Lele's notes (care notes)

Lele keeps notes so each session builds on the last:

- If something is sore or feels wrong during an exercise, Lele writes it down, with the exercise, the spot, how bad it was, and your own words.
- Next time you do that exercise, Lele makes it a little easier. For a bending exercise, a smaller bend counts as a full rep; for a hold, the hold is shorter. Lele tells you when it has done this.
- After 2 comfortable sessions in a row, Lele eases the exercise back towards normal a step at a time.
- If you tell Lele in the morning that things are worse than yesterday, the whole session that day is gentler, and your physio is told.
- Your physio sees every note, can keep an exercise easier, and can write notes for Lele to follow.
- You can see your notes in the Progress tab, and tell Lele when something feels better.

## Adding programs while you're in physio

- While a physio supervises you, they check anything you add. Pick a program in Programs → Explore and tap "Ask" to send your physio a request.
- Lele tells you their answer. If they say yes, the program is added, and your physio's limits still apply in it.
- Quick sessions are always open to you, with anything your limits rule out left out.

## Finishing physio

- Only your physio can end a physio program. When you're ready, they discharge you with a note.
- Your history stays. Any limits they want to keep still protect you in every program.
- Lele suggests a program to keep going with, such as Strong knees for life after knee physio, or Shoulder mobility after shoulder physio.

## What your physio sees

- Your program, your sessions and verified reps, and your sessions live while you're doing them.
- Your knee bend, arm lift and strength trends, and your check-in results.
- Pain ratings and every symptom you reported, with the exercise, rep and angle at the moment you said it.
- Lele's notes, your answers to Lele's check-ins, and a daily list of what Lele noticed.
- A pre-visit report to discuss at your next appointment.

## Rewards and habits

- **Daily check-in:** open the app and claim XP on a 7-day cycle that grows each day.
- **Streaks:** moving on consecutive days builds a streak. Shields protect it. The rule is "never miss twice": one missed day is fine, two in a row breaks it.
- **Levels, badges and milestones:** for example, reaching 90° of knee bend, lifting your arm to 120°, or your first full push-up.

## What Lele will not do

- It won't diagnose, or say what is wrong with you.
- It won't tell you to start, stop or change a medicine, or advise on doses or timing. Ask your doctor or pharmacist.
- It won't change your physio's program. Your physio can ask Lele to draft changes, and they sign them.
- It won't replace urgent care. For warning signs, Lele will tell you to stop and get help.
