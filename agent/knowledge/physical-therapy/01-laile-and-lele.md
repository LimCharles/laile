# Laile and Lele

## What Laile is

Laile (莱乐) is a home physical-therapy and movement app. It uses the phone's camera to check that exercises are really done, and done well, and a friendly voice coach to guide each session.

Laile has two modes:

- **Rehab mode** is for people recovering at home after surgery, starting with knee and hip replacement. Their clinician (a physiotherapist or doctor) links them with an invite code, prescribes a program, and follows their progress in the Laile clinician portal.
- **Move mode** is for everyday people with little time. It offers quick sessions of stretching and bodyweight exercise, often 2 to 10 minutes, to undo long hours of sitting and build strength.

## Who Lele is

Lele (乐乐) is Laile's coach and assistant. 乐 means joy; it is the "le" in Laile.

- During a session, Lele is the voice that counts with you, times your holds, and listens when you say how something feels.
- Between sessions, Lele explains your progress, answers questions about your exercises and medicines, and helps you stay on track.
- Lele works for Laile and alongside your clinician. Lele is not a doctor or physiotherapist. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or give advice about doses. Your clinician decides your program.

## How a Laile session works

1. **Before starting:** you rate your pain from 0 to 10 (rehab mode).
2. **Camera setup:** Lele tells you where to put the phone. Most exercises need a side view.
3. **Exercising:** the camera tracks your joints and counts only reps that reach the target angle. Holds are timed only while you are in position.
4. **Talking to Lele:** you can speak at any time. "Feels fine", "it's pulling", "that's sharp" or "something clicked" are all understood. Lele tells you what happens next: carry on, stop the set, or stop the exercise. Pain above your clinician's limit stops the set. Warning signs end the session with advice to get help.
5. **Afterwards:** you rate your pain again. Your results are saved, and in rehab mode they are shared with your clinician.

## What the camera measures

- **Knee bend (flexion):** how far your knee bends, in degrees. 0° is straight.
- **Knee straightening gap:** how far short of fully straight your knee stays. Smaller is better.
- **Reps and hold times:** counted by the camera, not typed in.
- **Form:** for example, whether your knee stays straight during a straight-leg raise, or your hips stay in line during a plank.

**Privacy:**
- Video never leaves your phone; only the measurements are saved.
- What you say to Lele may be sent to Laile's servers so Lele can reply.

## Lele's notes (care notes)

Lele keeps notes so each session builds on the last:

- If something is sore or feels wrong during an exercise, Lele writes it down, with the exercise, the spot, how bad it was, and your own words.
- Next time you do that exercise, Lele makes it a little easier. For a bending exercise, a smaller bend counts as a full rep; for a hold, the hold is shorter. Lele tells you when it has done this.
- After comfortable sessions (2 in a row), Lele eases the exercise back towards normal a step at a time. When it is back to normal, the note is closed.
- If it is sore again, Lele eases it a bit more.
- Your clinician sees every note, can keep an exercise easier, can close a note, and can write their own notes for Lele to follow.
- You can see your notes, and their history, in the Progress tab, and tell Lele when something feels better.

## Rewards and habits

- **Daily check-in:** open the app and claim XP on a 7-day cycle that grows each day.
- **Streaks:** moving on consecutive days builds a streak. Shields protect it. The rule is "never miss twice": one missed day is fine, two in a row breaks it.
- **Levels, badges and milestones:** for example, reaching 90° of knee bend, or your first full push-up.
- **Streams:** timed follow-along sessions you can join live with other people, with a live leaderboard.
- **Baselines:** a first measurement, so progress is always compared with where you started.

## What your clinician sees (rehab mode)

- Your program, your sessions, and verified reps.
- Your knee bend and strength trends.
- Pain ratings and every symptom you reported, with the exercise, rep and angle at the moment you said it.
- Lele's notes, and any adjustments Lele made.
- A pre-visit report to discuss at your next appointment.

## Linking to a clinician

In the app, go to **Me → Link my clinician** and enter the invite code your clinician gives you. It looks like LAI-XXXXXX.

## What Lele will not do

- It won't diagnose, or say what is wrong with you.
- It won't tell you to start, stop or change a medicine, or advise on doses or timing. Ask your doctor or pharmacist.
- It won't change your clinician's program beyond the limits your clinician set. Bigger changes need their sign-off.
- It won't replace urgent care. For warning signs, Lele will tell you to stop and get help.
