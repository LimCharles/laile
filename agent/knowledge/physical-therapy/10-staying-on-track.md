# Staying on track

Most people know exercise helps. The hard part is doing it on ordinary days. This guide covers the common things that get in the way, and what helps.

## First, ask what's getting in the way

When sessions are missed, Lele asks why before suggesting anything, because the answer changes what helps.

## Common barriers and what helps

### "I don't have time"
- Laile sessions can be very short. Even a 2–5 minute session counts towards your streak.
- Attach exercise to something you already do every day, like after brushing your teeth, before lunch, or while the kettle boils. This is called habit stacking.
- Leave your phone stand where you exercise, so setup takes seconds.

### "It hurts"
- Tell Lele exactly where and when. Lele notes it and eases that exercise next time, and your clinician sees it.
- Some discomfort is expected, but pain shouldn't be the reason you stop altogether. If pain keeps stopping you, your clinician may need to review your program or pain plan.

### "I'm worried I'll damage the new joint"
- Worry about moving after surgery is very common and understandable.
- Your exercises were chosen by your clinician to be safe at this stage. Laile checks every exercise against your precautions.
- Pulling and stretching sensations are expected; they usually mean the joint is regaining movement.
- Say how it feels as you go. Lele will stop you if something is outside your limits.

### "I forgot"
- Use reminders at the same time each day.
- The daily check-in reward is a nudge to open the app.
- Put your exercise spot somewhere you'll see it.

### "I'm not seeing progress"
- Look at your charts in the Progress tab. Laile compares you with your starting baseline, and progress often comes in steps.
- Milestones mark the moments that matter, like 90° of knee bend or your first full push-up.

### "I'm tired or low"
- A shorter, gentler session still counts. Try the gentle option on the Today screen.
- If low mood is lasting, talk to your doctor. In Singapore, Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) is on 1767, 24 hours.

### "It's boring"
- Try a stream: a timed follow-along session with other people and a live leaderboard.
- Earn XP, level up, and collect badges.

## Streaks without guilt: "never miss twice"

- Missing one day is normal and fine.
- The aim is not to miss two days in a row.
- Shields protect your streak on the odd day you can't.

## Preparing for your next appointment

- Laile builds a pre-visit report for your clinician.
- Before a visit, write down: what's improved; what's been hard; any pain that worries you; questions about your medicines or activities.
- Lele can help you put your questions together.
