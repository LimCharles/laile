Laile privacy policy

Laile (莱乐) is a home physiotherapy and exercise app. Updated 11 October 2026.

What Laile stores

Your account (name, email and a hashed password), the programs you follow, the results of each session (reps, holds, joint angles and how long it took), what you tell Lele about pain or how an exercise felt, your care notes, any medicines and reminders you add, and your messages with Lele.

What stays on your phone

The camera video used to count reps and measure joint angles is processed on your iPhone and is never uploaded or saved. Only the numbers it produces are sent.

Who processes it

  • Laile's server on Tencent Cloud in Singapore stores your records.
  • Tencent Cloud ADP receives your chat messages, with the program and session context Lele needs, to write Lele's replies.
  • ElevenLabs turns Lele's own lines into speech. Your voice is not sent to ElevenLabs.
  • Apple provides speech recognition when you talk to Lele during a session.
  • Your physio, if you joined with their code, sees your program, results, symptom reports and notes.

What Laile does not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.

Your choices

You can stop using Laile at any time. To have your account and records deleted, send a request through the feedback option in TestFlight. Demo accounts are reset regularly.

About this version

This is a test build for the Tencent Cloud AI CAN DO IT Hackathon 2026. It is meant for trying the app, not for managing real medical care.
