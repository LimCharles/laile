Your physio signs the program. The camera counts every rep and measures how far your knee bends. Lele, the coach, remembers how you're doing, plans with you, and writes your physio a brief before each visit.

How it works

Physio

A program the physio signs

Lele drafts it from the referral. Laile's code checks it against the physio's limits, and the physio edits and signs it in the portal.

Camera

Every rep counted

Pose tracking on the phone or in the browser counts reps, times holds and measures joint angles. Video never leaves the device.

Lele

A coach that remembers

Say "ow, sharp, inside of my knee" mid-set and the exercise stops, the moment is logged with the knee angle, and next time it's eased.

Goals

Plan with Lele in chat

Tell Lele your new goals and time. Lele drafts a program. Physio patients' plans go to their physio to approve first.

Portal

A brief for the physio

Lele summarises adherence, measurements and what the person reported, with what's typical at this stage from the knowledge base.

Calisthenics

Strength at home too

From your first push-up to squats that carry you up any stairs: Laile's programs check in first, then set targets that progress with you.
